# Technical Status — BikerGram V2.0

## Frontend
- Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
- Existing template pages retained and restyled through `assets/bikergram-v2.css`.
- New pages share navigation/footer and visual components.
- Fixture data exposed as `window.BIKERGRAM_FIXTURE`.

## Not implemented
- API/server and database.
- Authentication and account persistence.
- Email verification and reset emails.
- Story and business-request submission storage.
- Moderation/admin workflows.
- Notifications delivery.
- Actual upload storage.
- Production cookie-consent storage/audit.
- Verified map/geocoding/route services.

## Data integrity
- Demo data is fictional.
- Districts are incomplete sample records.
- No route geometry or verified coordinates supplied.
- Contact data/social handles must not be fabricated.
